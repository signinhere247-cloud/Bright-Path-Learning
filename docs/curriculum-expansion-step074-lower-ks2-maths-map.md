# Step 74 of 250 — Lower KS2 mathematics progression map

Adds `curriculum/lower-ks2-maths-progression-map.json` with separate Year 3 and Year 4 learning progressions across eight domains: number/place value, addition/subtraction, multiplication/division, fractions, measurement, geometry, statistics and reasoning.

The map records key progression milestones, including three-digit to four-digit number understanding, Year 3 3/4/8 tables to Year 4 all tables to 12 × 12, tenths to hundredths/equivalent fractions, and pictograms/bar charts to line graphs.

**Review gate:** this is a working planning map based on the broad England primary mathematics programme. Before it is used as a statutory coverage checklist, educators must verify every statement against the current official programme of study and any relevant updates, correct wording, sequence, and map individual objectives to lessons and assessments. It is not an approved scheme of work, and no live lesson integration is included.
