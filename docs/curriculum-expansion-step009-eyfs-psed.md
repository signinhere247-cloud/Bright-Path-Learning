## Existing lesson review
The real `src/earlyDevelopment.js` bank provides 10 multiple-choice questions in each of these four topics (40 total):
- Naming feelings
- Calming and self-regulation
- Friendship and kindness
- Hands and independence

These introduce helpful ideas, but answering hypothetical scenarios is **not evidence** of a child's emotional regulation, confidence, independence, social relationships or self-care in everyday life.

## Safeguards for implementation
- Avoid scores, leaderboards, penalties or BrightCoins for expressing emotions, regulating behaviour or meeting self-care milestones.
- Do not treat quiz results as clinical or developmental assessment.
- Allow AAC, sign, gesture, pictures and alternative communication; do not assume eye contact, speech or a particular social style is necessary.
- Include sensory-friendly, neurodiversity-affirming and culturally respectful approaches.
- Activities should support co-regulation and choice, not demand compliance or suppress harmless self-regulatory behaviour.
- No child photos or sensitive observations should be required.

**Current status:** Four strands have candidate quiz content and proposed adult-supported activities; **zero** have verified real-world learning evidence or educator review.
