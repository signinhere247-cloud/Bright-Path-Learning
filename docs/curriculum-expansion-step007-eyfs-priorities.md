# Step 7 of 250 — EYFS curriculum review and prioritisation

## Reproducible queue
Run `node scripts/eyfs-review-queue.mjs`. This generates **28 review tasks** from the Step 6 traceability register, grouped into editorial delivery priorities.

| Priority | Learning areas | Tasks |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| P0 | Communication and language, physical development, literacy | 12 |
| P1 | Mathematics, personal/social/emotional development | 8 |
| P2 | Understanding the world, expressive arts and design | 8 |

**Important:** P2 does **not** mean unimportant, optional or less statutory. All seven EYFS areas must be supported. This is an operational order for inspecting existing lessons and planning new activity guides, not a ranking of children's needs.

Every review task requires inspecting current lessons, checking the proposed hands-on activity, reviewing accessibility, checking safety and privacy, and obtaining an educator's review. All tasks remain **awaiting review** and none is marked as verified curriculum coverage.

## Suggested delivery order
1. Inspect the current communication and language lessons for actual conversational opportunities.
2. Inspect physical development for authentic offline movement and fine-motor tasks.
3. Inspect early literacy for shared reading, phonics, mark-making and writing.
4. Continue through every remaining EYFS area without omitting creative or world learning.

## Step 8 hand-off
Begin detailed evidence review of communication and language, including exact lesson links and practical adult-child conversation guidance. Do not certify coverage without educational review.
