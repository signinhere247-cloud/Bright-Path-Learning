# Step 60 of 250 — KS1 science expansion

Adds four supplementary adult-supported investigations in `src/ks1ScienceActivities.js`. Year 1: identifying plant parts and comparing material properties. Year 2: living/dead/never-alive sorting and animals' basic needs.

Each guide has materials, four practical steps, a scientific discussion prompt and specific safety notes. The activities are **not yet wired into live learner navigation** and do not store observations or certify mastery.

These four examples do not exhaust KS1 science: seasonal changes, everyday plants and growing, animals and humans, uses of materials, food chains, habitats, and working scientifically need further coverage and educator review. Statutory objective-by-objective alignment remains unverified.
