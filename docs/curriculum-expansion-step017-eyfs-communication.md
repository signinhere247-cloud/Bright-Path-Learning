# Step 17 of 250 — EYFS communication play activities

Extends the live `EYFS Play Activities` interface with:
- **Rhythm and rhyme play** (ACT-EYFS-P1-03): shared rhyme, rhythm, pausing and responsive participation.
- **Make-believe story** (ACT-EYFS-P1-04): child-led character choice, story events and endings.

Combined with Step 16, **all four planned Communication and Language activity guides** are now implemented in the in-app selector. Each provides four practical adult-supported steps, a conversation prompt, safety advice and speech-free participation options.

The activities are optional, ungraded and do not collect recordings, photos or sensitive child data. Educator verification, observation evidence and individual child progress integration are still outstanding; no statutory EYFS coverage is certified.
