# Step 23 of 250 — EYFS Expressive Arts and Design play activities

Adds four child-led adult-supported activity guides to the existing in-app EYFS selector:
1. **Materials studio** (ACT-EYFS-P7-01): open-ended safe making and exploring materials.
2. **Pretend-play corner** (ACT-EYFS-P7-02): imaginative role-play with inclusive props and choice.
3. **Music and movement** (ACT-EYFS-P7-03): gentle rhythm and optional sound, movement or silent patterns.
4. **Share and appreciate** (ACT-EYFS-P7-04): responding to age-appropriate art, music and performance without compulsory disclosure.

**All 28 of 28 editorially planned EYFS activity guides** are now implemented across seven learning areas. The test suite checks all 28 identifiers, guide fields, and seven learning areas. This completes the planned *activity-guide backlog*, **not** statutory EYFS coverage or proof of child mastery.

Safeguards: adult supervision, non-toxic materials, no small choking hazards, comfortable sound levels, accessible sensory/motor alternatives, no scoring and no personal data collection.

Outstanding work includes browser checks on live deployment, educator and safeguarding review, child-profile progress integration, and validation against all 17 statutory ELGs and the wider educational programmes.
