# Step 11 of 250 — EYFS literacy review

## Existing module inspected
`src/earlyLiteracy.js` contains **8 lessons with 10 multiple-choice questions each**:
Letters and names; Beginning sounds; Rhyming words; Blend the sounds; Common words; Read a sentence; Capital letters; Writing and punctuation.

The lessons provide structured prompts and explanations, but this audit does not establish that a child can decode aloud, understand a story, form letters, write for a purpose or enjoy shared reading. Screen-based selection is not the same as authentic reading and writing.

## Four programme strands
| Strand | Candidate content | Missing experience |
|---|---|---|
| EYFS-P4-01 | Read a sentence; Rhyming words | Shared books, story meaning, child-led discussion |
| EYFS-P4-02 | Read a sentence | Retelling stories with pictures, props or actions |
| EYFS-P4-03 | Beginning sounds; Blend the sounds; Common words; Letters and names | Educator-verified systematic phonics progression and sound-to-print practice |
| EYFS-P4-04 | Writing and punctuation; Capital letters | Actual mark-making, meaningful writing, adapted pencil or alternative input |

## Design checks
- Review phonics sequencing against current DfE guidance and the setting's chosen systematic synthetic phonics approach; don't infer a complete programme from a few quizzes.
- Provide shared reading and oral language experiences, not only word recognition.
- Offer child-created marks, letters, drawing, dictated text and accessible writing tools.
- Ensure EAL, SEND and alternative communication pathways are supported without penalising children.
- Keep screen sessions short and pair activities with adult-led offline experiences.
- Do not treat quiz results as formal assessment or certify statutory coverage without educator review.

**Result:** Four strands have candidate lessons; **zero** are verified as complete.
