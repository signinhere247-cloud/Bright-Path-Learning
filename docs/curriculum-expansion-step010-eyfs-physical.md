## Safety and inclusion requirements
- An adult should choose age-appropriate, safe environments and supervise any tools or small objects.
- Avoid choking hazards, unsafe heights, sharp tools and compulsory strenuous activity.
- Offer seated, wheelchair-accessible, adapted-grip and sensory-friendly alternatives.
- Never score a child's motor ability, diagnose delays or compare children on leaderboards.
- Make activities short, playful and optional; no video or photo proof is required.
- Provide clear stop-if-uncomfortable instructions and respect the child's preferences.
