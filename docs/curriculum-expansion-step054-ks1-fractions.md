# Step 54 of 250 — KS1 fractions

Adds four adult-supported practical fraction activities in `src/ks1FractionsActivities.js`: Year 1 halves and quarters of shapes; Year 2 fractions of quantities, thirds and equivalence. Every guide has an example, four hands-on steps and a reasoning prompt.

These are supplementary activities, not a complete fractions curriculum. They do not store learner answers or certify mastery and are **not yet connected to the live learning interface**. Educator review and objective-by-objective statutory alignment remain outstanding.
