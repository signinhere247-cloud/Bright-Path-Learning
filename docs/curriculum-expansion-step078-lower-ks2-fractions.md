# Step 78 of 250 — Lower KS2 fractions

Adds `src/lowerKs2FractionsLessons.js` with eight supplementary lessons: four for Year 3 and four for Year 4. Each includes an explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with three distinct options, correct answers and explanatory feedback.

## Year 3
1. Unit and non-unit fractions
2. Counting in tenths
3. Fractions of amounts
4. Comparing, adding and subtracting fractions with the same denominator

## Year 4
1. Equivalent fractions
2. Counting in hundredths and linking fractions to decimals
3. Adding and subtracting fractions with the same denominator
4. Fractions of quantities and contextual problems

A year-specific retrieval helper returns copies. Tests check year separation, lesson and question counts, unique options, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** draft supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, suitable representations, accessibility, progression and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are **not yet connected to learner navigation** and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
