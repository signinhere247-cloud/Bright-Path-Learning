# Step 63 of 250 — KS1 art and design

Adds four adult-supported practical art activities in `src/ks1ArtDesignActivities.js`: Year 1 drawing lines/textures and mixing paint; Year 2 sculpture construction and comparing artwork/techniques.

Each guide has materials, four creative steps, an adult discussion question and safety guidance. The art comparison activity requires properly licensed and age-appropriate source reproductions; no reproductions are included.

These are supplementary activity guides, not a complete art curriculum or verified assessment. No child artwork is uploaded or stored. They are **not yet wired into live learner navigation**. Educator review, progressive sketchbook practice and statutory objective mapping remain outstanding.
