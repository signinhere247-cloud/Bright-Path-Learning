# Step 65 of 250 — KS1 computing

Adds four adult-supported activities in `src/ks1ComputingActivities.js`. Year 1: creating a sequence of instructions and debugging an incorrect sequence. Year 2: designing digital content for a fictional audience and discussing privacy, reporting and trusted adults online.

Activities are offline by default and include materials, four guided steps, a reasoning prompt and privacy/safety notes. No passwords, real child identifiers or disclosures are collected.

These are supplementary guides, not a complete KS1 computing curriculum. They are **not yet wired into live learner navigation**. Practical programming on devices, digital research, safe technology use, educator review and statutory objective mapping remain outstanding.
