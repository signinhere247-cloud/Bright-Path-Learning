# Step 21 of 250 — EYFS mathematics practical activities

1. **Count real treasures** (ACT-EYFS-P5-01): one-to-one counting and comparing groups with safe large objects.
2. **Build number groups** (ACT-EYFS-P5-02): represent small quantities using blocks, dots or fingers.
3. **Shape detectives** (ACT-EYFS-P5-03): explore shapes, size and position in familiar surroundings.
4. **Pattern maker** (ACT-EYFS-P5-04): make and extend repeating patterns with objects, images or gentle movements.
