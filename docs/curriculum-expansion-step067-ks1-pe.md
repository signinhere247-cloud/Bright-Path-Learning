# Step 67 of 250 — KS1 physical education

Adds four adult-supported PE activities in `src/ks1PhysicalEducationActivities.js`. Year 1: balance pathway and controlled direction changes. Year 2: expressive movement sequences and a cooperative beanbag target game.

Activities include four guided steps, materials, adult reflection prompts, non-competitive options, sensory/physical accessibility adjustments and safety notes. They are not medical or physiotherapy instructions; adults should adapt to each learner's capabilities and environment.

These are supplementary guides, not verified attainment assessments. No physical-performance data is collected, and the activities are **not yet wired into live learner navigation**. Educator review, swimming (outside KS1 statutory PE requirements), broader games/dance progression and objective-level mapping remain outstanding.
