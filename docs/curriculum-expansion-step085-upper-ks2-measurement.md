# Step 85 of 250 — Upper KS2 measurement and unit conversions

Adds ten supplementary Year 5 and Year 6 lessons covering metric length, mass and capacity conversions; perimeter and area; time and timetables; approximate metric-imperial equivalences; volume of cubes and cuboids; and multi-step measurement problems.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, appropriate diagrams and measurement representations, and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
