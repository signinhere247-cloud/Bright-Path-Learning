# Step 75 of 250 — Lower KS2 number and place value

Adds `src/lowerKs2NumberPlaceValueLessons.js` with eight supplementary lessons: four for Year 3 and four for Year 4. Each lesson contains an explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with three distinct options, correct answers and explanatory feedback.

## Year 3
1. Hundreds, tens and ones
2. Compare and order to 1,000
3. Count in equal steps (4, 8, 50 and 100)
4. Find 10 or 100 more and less

## Year 4
1. Thousands, hundreds, tens and ones
2. Compare and order to 10,000
3. Round to the nearest 10, 100 and 1,000
4. Roman numerals to 100

A year-specific retrieval helper returns copies of lesson data. Automated tests check lesson counts, question structure, unique options, answer presence, feedback and year restrictions.

**Review gate:** content is supplementary draft material. Educators must check mathematical accuracy, wording, accessibility, progression and exact statutory objective mapping. These lessons are **not yet wired into learner navigation**, and completing the module does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
