# Step 66 of 250 — KS1 music

Adds four adult-supported practical activities in `src/ks1MusicActivities.js`. Year 1: clap/rest rhythms and pitch discrimination. Year 2: composing an original eight-beat pattern and comparing contrasting short excerpts.

Each guide has materials, four steps, an adult question and hearing/sensory accessibility notes. Any music excerpts must be original or appropriately licensed; none are bundled and no audio is played by this module.

These supplementary guides do not store recordings, certify mastery or complete the KS1 music curriculum. They are **not yet wired into live learner navigation**. Educator review, singing, instrumental technique, ensemble performance and objective-level mapping remain outstanding.
