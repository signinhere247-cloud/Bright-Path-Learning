# Step 48 of 250 — KS1 writing and composition

Adds four adult-supported original writing activities to `src/ks1WritingActivities.js`: Year 1 simple sentences and description, Year 2 short stories and instructions. Each includes a writing prompt, four practical steps and three feedback checklist items.

Activities encourage oral rehearsal, punctuation, spelling awareness, handwriting and revision. No child writing is collected or stored, and no automated writing assessment is claimed. The new module is **not yet integrated into the live lesson UI** and requires educator review for curriculum alignment.
