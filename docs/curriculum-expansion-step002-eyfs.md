# Step 2 of 250 — England EYFS 2026 framework mapping

**Authoritative source:** Department for Education, EYFS statutory framework for group and school-based providers, effective 1 September 2026: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/early-years-foundation-stage-framework--2

The 2026 framework describes **seven areas of learning**, comprising three prime and four specific areas. The **17 early learning goals (ELGs)** are used for a holistic end-of-EYFS assessment, **not** as the curriculum itself. Children aged 3–4 need rich, play-based experiences rather than tests against end-of-Reception goals.

## Priorities for subsequent steps
1. Extract the **educational programme requirements** for each of the seven areas, not merely the 17 ELG titles.
2. Connect programme experiences to age-appropriate guided play, discussion, physical tasks, creative expression and observation.
3. Audit actual lesson content and questions; identify missing teaching, age-appropriate activities and evidence types.
4. Add parent-led offline activities where screens or multiple-choice questions are unsuitable.
5. Review all mappings against the current framework with an early-years education specialist.

## Acceptance and limitations
This step establishes the **official framework structure and an initial candidate mapping**. It does not certify compliance with the full EYFS educational programmes, nor should the app claim to assess children officially against the EYFS Profile.
