# Step 77 of 250 — Lower KS2 multiplication and division

Adds `src/lowerKs2MultiplicationDivisionLessons.js` with eight supplementary lessons: four for Year 3 and four for Year 4. Each includes an explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with three distinct options, correct answers and explanatory feedback.

## Year 3
1. Recall the 3, 4 and 8 times tables
2. Use related multiplication and division facts
3. Multiply a two-digit number by a one-digit number
4. Solve multiplication and division problems, including scaling and correspondence

## Year 4
1. Recall all multiplication tables to 12 × 12
2. Multiply two-digit and three-digit numbers by one digit
3. Divide up to three-digit numbers by one digit and interpret remainders
4. Solve multiplication and division problems in context

A year-specific retrieval helper returns copies. Tests check year separation, lesson/question counts, unique options, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** draft supplementary content must be checked by educators for arithmetic, wording, progression, accessibility and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are **not yet connected to learner navigation** and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
