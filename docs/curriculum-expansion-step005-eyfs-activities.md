# Step 5 of 250 — EYFS missing learning activities and objectives

## Delivered
Created `curriculum/eyfs-activity-backlog.json` with **28 specific proposed adult-supported activities**, one for each Step 3 EYFS educational-programme teaching strand. Each includes a stable ID, practical instruction, learning mode, optional observational evidence, accessibility expectations and an explicit **not implemented** status.

Examples include shared reading, conversation circles, safe outdoor movement, fine-motor play, real-object counting, nature observation, child-led art and musical exploration.

## Why this is necessary
The existing early-years quiz modules do not on their own provide the rich play, practical experiences, creative expression and adult interaction required for high-quality early learning. These activities are **requirements for future implementation**, not lessons already available to families.

## Next implementation requirements
- Present each activity as a short parent/guardian guide, optionally linked to a child's current EYFS learning area.
- Avoid using BrightCoins or quiz scores as a measure of emotional, physical or developmental achievement.
- Do not collect identifiable child photographs, medical details or developmental diagnoses to prove completion.
- Offer accessible alternatives for different abilities, languages and communication styles.
- Keep optional parent reflections private and child-specific, and preserve existing saved progress.
- Verify the educational design and curriculum links before marking any strand as complete.
