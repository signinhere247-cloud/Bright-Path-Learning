# Step 87 of 250 — Upper KS2 position and direction

Adds ten supplementary Year 5 and Year 6 lessons covering first-quadrant coordinates, horizontal and vertical movement, translations, reflections, compass directions and turns, coordinates in all four quadrants, coordinate patterns, reflections in the axes, coordinate translations and multi-step position problems.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking, coordinate-grid diagrams and practical mapping activities, and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
