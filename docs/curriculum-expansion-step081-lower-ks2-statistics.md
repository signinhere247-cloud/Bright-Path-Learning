# Step 81 of 250 — Lower KS2 statistics and data handling

Adds eight supplementary Year 3 and Year 4 lessons covering data collection, tally and frequency tables, pictograms, bar charts, scaled axes, time graphs, and solving comparison and total problems.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, accessible chart representations, suitable data sets and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
