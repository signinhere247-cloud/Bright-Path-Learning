## Actual lesson evidence
Inspected `src/earlyDevelopment.js`. The existing learning bank includes:
- **Listening and attention:** 10 multiple-choice questions about listening, instructions and sounds.
- **Speaking and storytelling:** 10 multiple-choice questions about speaking, describing and story structure.
- **Understanding instructions:** 10 multiple-choice questions about one- and two-step directions.

All 30 questions are generated with the generic explanation pattern `Talk with a grown-up: [correct answer].` These questions provide useful discussion prompts but do **not** prove authentic listening, child-led speaking, vocabulary application, conversation turns or oral storytelling.

## Design and inclusion checks
Offer speech, gesture, AAC, sign and picture-based responses; avoid penalising speech differences, EAL or disability. Never infer a developmental diagnosis from quiz answers. Keep adult guidance short and flexible, with optional offline participation and no forced uploads.
