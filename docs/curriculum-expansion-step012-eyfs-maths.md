# Step 12 of 250 — EYFS mathematics review

## Existing lessons inspected
The `src/earlyMaths.js` module contains eight lessons with ten questions each: Count to ten; Number order; More and fewer; Adding together; Taking away; Shapes around us; Repeating patterns; Size and measures.

The 80 questions provide a foundation for discussing maths, but selecting answers on a screen is not evidence of counting objects with one-to-one correspondence, subitising, composing numbers, describing patterns or reasoning spatially.

## Quality and inclusion checks
- Prioritise physical manipulatives and playful adult-supported tasks over written calculations.
- Review age appropriateness: formal units such as millilitres or metres should not be required mastery for 3–5-year-olds.
- Provide accessible large objects, tactile options, audio/visual prompts and alternative response methods.
- Avoid small loose parts where there is a choking risk; adults select safe materials.
- Check mathematical explanations, misconceptions and developmental sequence with an educator.
- Do not certify statutory EYFS coverage based on the presence of a quiz.

**Result:** Four strands have candidate lessons and planned practical activities; **zero** have verified hands-on evidence or educator review.
