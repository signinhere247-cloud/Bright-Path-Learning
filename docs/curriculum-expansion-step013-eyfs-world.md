## Design and safety requirements
- Invite safe outdoor or indoor nature observation with adult supervision; avoid handling unknown plants, animals or substances.
- Avoid stereotypes and tokenism. Include different families, faiths, languages and communities respectfully.
- Encourage observation, predictions and questions rather than expecting one rigid answer to every exploratory question.
- Offer accessible indoor alternatives for mobility, sensory and weather barriers.
- Do not request children's home address, location, family photos or identifying personal details.
- Seek educational review before certifying curriculum coverage.
