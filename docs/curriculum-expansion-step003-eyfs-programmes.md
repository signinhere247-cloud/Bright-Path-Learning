## Design safeguards
- Children aged 3–5 need play, hands-on exploration and adult interaction; screen-based multiple-choice questions are supplemental, not substitutes.
- The early learning goals are end-of-Reception assessment reference points, not the curriculum itself.
- Plan inclusive adaptations and varied developmental pacing, including SEND and English as an additional language.
- In line with the September 2026 framework, consider age-appropriate screen use; avoid encouraging long sessions.
- No goal or strand is claimed fully covered until linked lessons, teaching activities and evidence have been reviewed.
