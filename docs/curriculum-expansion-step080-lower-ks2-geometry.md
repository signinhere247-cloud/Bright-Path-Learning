# Step 80 of 250 — Lower KS2 geometry

Adds eight supplementary Year 3 and Year 4 lessons covering 2-D shapes, 3-D shapes, constructing shapes, right angles and turns, classifying quadrilaterals, symmetry, comparing angles, and classifying triangles and quadrilaterals.

Each lesson includes an explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, correct answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests check lesson and question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** draft supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, suitable diagrams and representations, accessibility, progression and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.

The Year 4 retrieval test uses the Year 4 symmetry lesson (LKS2-GE-Y4-02) to verify year-specific content.
