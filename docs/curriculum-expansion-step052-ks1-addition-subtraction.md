# Step 52 of 250 — KS1 addition and subtraction

Adds four adult-supported practical maths activities in `src/ks1AdditionSubtractionActivities.js`: Year 1 bonds to ten and missing-number subtraction; Year 2 two-digit addition strategies and contextual subtraction. Each includes a worked example, four concrete practice steps and an explanation prompt.

These are supplementary teaching guides, not a comprehensive sequence or assessment. They do not store results or certify mastery, and are **not yet connected to live learner navigation**. Formal statutory objective mapping and educator review remain outstanding.
