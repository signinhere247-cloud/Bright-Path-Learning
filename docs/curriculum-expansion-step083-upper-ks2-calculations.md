# Step 83 of 250 — Upper KS2 addition, subtraction, multiplication and division

Adds ten supplementary Year 5 and Year 6 lessons covering formal addition and subtraction, short multiplication and division, long multiplication and division, factors and multiples, prime numbers, order of operations and multi-step word problems.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, accessible written-method demonstrations and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
