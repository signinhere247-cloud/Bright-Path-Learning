# Step 55 of 250 — KS1 measurement

Adds four adult-supported measurement guides to `src/ks1MeasurementActivities.js`: Year 1 length and mass comparisons; Year 2 centimetres/metres, capacity and time. Activities include physical materials, four guided steps and adult reasoning prompts.

The guides are supplementary, not a complete measurement curriculum. They do not store results, automatically assess practical measurement skills or certify mastery. They are **not yet integrated into live learner navigation**. Educator review and objective-by-objective statutory mapping remain outstanding.
