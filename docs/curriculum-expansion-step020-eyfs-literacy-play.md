# Step 20 of 250 — EYFS literacy play activities

Four literacy activity guides are now added to the existing in-app EYFS selector:
1. **Choose a story** (ACT-EYFS-P4-01): child-chosen books, pictures and shared reading.
2. **Tell it back** (ACT-EYFS-P4-02): imaginative retelling with props, pictures or actions.
3. **Sound hunt** (ACT-EYFS-P4-03): playful awareness of initial sounds; letters only when developmentally appropriate.
4. **Write for a purpose** (ACT-EYFS-P4-04): meaningful cards, lists and signs through marks, pictures, dictation or letters.

**16 of 28 planned EYFS activity guides** are now coded across four areas. They are optional and adult-supported, with accessible communication and motor alternatives and no grading or uploads.

These activity guides are not evidence of independent literacy mastery. Educator review, browser accessibility checks, child-profile integration and the remaining twelve guides are still outstanding.
