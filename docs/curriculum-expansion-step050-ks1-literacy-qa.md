# Step 50 of 250 — KS1 literacy quality review

Automated cross-module structural QA for four supplementary KS1 literacy modules: phonics, reading, writing and spelling/handwriting. **16 activities total** (eight Year 1, eight Year 2). The QA checks activity counts, unique IDs, year grouping and required titles.

Run `node scripts/ks1-literacy-qa.mjs`.

Passing technical checks **does not** establish curriculum completeness. Open gates include educator review, statutory objective mapping, real-world reading/writing assessment, spoken-language provision, accessibility review and integration into the live learner experience.
