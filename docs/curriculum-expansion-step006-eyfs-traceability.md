# Step 6 of 250 — EYFS learning requirement traceability

Run `node scripts/eyfs-traceability.mjs` to generate a machine-readable register joining the 28 Step 3 educational-programme planning strands to the 28 Step 5 proposed practical activities.

## Status definitions
- **Planned:** A proposed adult-supported activity exists in the backlog.
- **Implemented:** An actual working activity and lesson link have been verified in BrightPath (none established by this step).
- **Reviewed:** An educator has checked educational accuracy, developmental appropriateness and statutory alignment (none established by this step).
- **Verified coverage:** The requirement has lesson evidence and has passed educational, accessibility and safeguarding reviews (none established by this step).

## Current baseline
- 28 of 28 planning strands have a proposed activity.
- 0 of 28 have verified lesson evidence or completed educational review.
- 0 of 28 can be certified as fully covered.

These 28 strands are editorial audit units, **not the statutory framework's count of learning objectives**. Parent observations are optional, flexible and should not be treated as developmental diagnoses or formal EYFS assessment.

## Evidence needed to close a gap
1. Link to a working lesson or adult activity page and demonstrate it functions.
2. Check educational accuracy and appropriate developmental sequence.
3. Provide meaningful hands-on, play-based and inclusive participation options.
4. Check safety, privacy, accessibility and age-appropriate screen use.
5. Have an educator review the link to the official EYFS framework before changing coverage status.

## Step 7
Create a curriculum review queue ordered by impact, age-stage suitability, practical evidence needs and gaps in the current lessons.
