# Step 79 of 250 — Lower KS2 measurement

Adds eight supplementary Year 3 and Year 4 lessons covering length, mass, capacity, perimeter, area and time. Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback.

## Year 3
1. Measure and compare lengths
2. Measure mass and capacity
3. Understand perimeter
4. Tell the time and calculate durations

## Year 4
1. Convert units of length
2. Find perimeter and area
3. Convert units of mass and capacity
4. Work with time and timetables

A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies. Tests check lesson and question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** draft supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, suitable representations, accessibility, progression and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
