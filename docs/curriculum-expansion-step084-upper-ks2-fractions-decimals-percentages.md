# Step 84 of 250 — Upper KS2 fractions, decimals and percentages

Adds ten supplementary Year 5 and Year 6 lessons covering equivalent fractions, comparing and ordering fractions, fraction calculations, decimal place value and rounding, multiplying and dividing fractions, percentage equivalences and percentage-of-amount problems.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, visual fraction models, progression and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
