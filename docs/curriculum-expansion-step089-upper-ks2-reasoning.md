# Step 89/250 — Upper KS2 reasoning and problem-solving

Adds ten supplementary Year 5/6 lessons: operation selection, patterns, estimation/inverse checks, multi-step money, interpreting remainders, fractions and percentages, scaling, algebraic thinking, counterexamples, and complex word problems. Each has an explanation, two worked examples and five three-option questions with feedback.

**Quality gate:** educator review is required, including curriculum alignment, checking every worked example and adding practical/visual representations. These are supplementary modules and are **not yet connected to pupil navigation, progress, or BrightCoins**. Automated tests validate structure, not mathematical accuracy.
