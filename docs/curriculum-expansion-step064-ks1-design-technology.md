# Step 64 of 250 — KS1 design and technology

Adds four adult-supported practical activities in `src/ks1DesignTechnologyActivities.js`. Year 1: designing a useful container and testing a paper bridge. Year 2: building a slider/lever picture and planning a healthy snack using pictures.

Each guide includes a design purpose, materials, four steps, an evaluation prompt and safety guidance. Food preparation is planning-only unless an adult separately supervises allergy-aware hygiene and preparation.

These are supplementary activities, not a complete DT curriculum or assessment. No child work is stored, and the guides are **not yet connected to live learner navigation**. Educator review and statutory objective-by-objective mapping remain outstanding.
