# Step 76 of 250 — Lower KS2 addition and subtraction

Adds `src/lowerKs2AdditionSubtractionLessons.js` with eight supplementary lessons: four for Year 3 and four for Year 4. Each includes an explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with three distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback.

## Year 3
1. Mental addition and subtraction
2. Column addition
3. Column subtraction with exchange
4. Estimate and check answers using inverse operations

## Year 4
1. Mental strategies with larger numbers
2. Column addition with four digits
3. Column subtraction with four digits and exchange
4. Two-step problems and checking
