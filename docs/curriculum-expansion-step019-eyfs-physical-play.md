# Step 19 of 250 — EYFS physical development play activities

Four adult-supported guides are now added to the in-app activity selector:
1. **Outdoor movement trail** (ACT-EYFS-P3-01): accessible route exploration and chosen movement.
2. **Balance and space game** (ACT-EYFS-P3-02): comfortable reaching, stretching and spatial vocabulary.
3. **Small hands workshop** (ACT-EYFS-P3-03): safe large-piece manipulation and tactile play.
4. **Draw and make marks** (ACT-EYFS-P3-04): child-led lines, marks and creative motor practice.

**12 of 28 planned EYFS activity guides** are now coded across communication, PSED and physical development. Activities are optional and ungraded, and offer seated, supported, adapted-grip or sensory-friendly participation.

Adult supervision is required; avoid choking hazards, unstable obstacles, unsafe heights, pressure to jump and tasks that cause discomfort. No physical skill is assessed through quiz answers.
