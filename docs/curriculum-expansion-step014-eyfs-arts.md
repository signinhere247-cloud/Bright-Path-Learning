## Inclusion and safety
- Offer multiple modes: drawing, modelling, music, movement, storytelling, role play, gesture and AAC.
- Allow sensory-friendly materials, quiet participation and adapted tools.
- Avoid scoring originality, artistic quality or emotional expression; rewards should not depend on adult approval of the result.
- Adult supervision is required for age-appropriate materials; avoid sharp tools, unsafe substances and choking hazards.
- Don't require photos, recordings or uploading a child's artwork.
- Obtain educational review and evidence of working child-led activities before claiming coverage.
