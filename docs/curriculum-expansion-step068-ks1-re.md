# Step 68 of 250 — KS1 religious education and worldviews

Adds four inclusive adult-supported enquiry activities in `src/ks1ReligiousEducationActivities.js`. Year 1: places of worship and meaningful places, celebrations and belonging. Year 2: values in stories and respectful dialogue about different beliefs and non-religious worldviews.

Activities avoid requiring personal belief disclosure, ranking beliefs or suggesting any single community is uniform. Photos and story resources must be sourced, accurately contextualised, licensed and educator-reviewed before use.

**RE in England is normally governed by a locally agreed syllabus or school-specific arrangements rather than a single uniform national curriculum programme of study.** These guides do not claim compliance with any specific local syllabus. They are **not yet connected to live learner navigation** and do not store personal data or certify attainment.
