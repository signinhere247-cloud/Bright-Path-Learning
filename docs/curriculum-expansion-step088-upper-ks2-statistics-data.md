# Step 88 of 250 — Upper KS2 statistics and data handling

Adds ten supplementary Year 5 and Year 6 lessons covering tables and timetables, comparison and totals, line graphs, collecting and organising data, choosing displays, pie charts, mean average, range and multi-step statistical reasoning.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking, accessible graph and chart visuals, practical data investigations and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
