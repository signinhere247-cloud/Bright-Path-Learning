# BrightPath Learning — official 250-step curriculum expansion roadmap

**Status:** Approved plan; work not yet complete. **Scope:** England EYFS, National Curriculum and selected GCSE specifications for ages 3–16. This is a planning estimate, not a claim of full coverage.

## Ground rules

- Each step must have a defined deliverable, acceptance criteria, tests, and an auditable evidence link.
- Maintain existing child profiles, cloud/local saves, BrightCoins, avatars, pets and homes. Avoid destructive migrations.
- Keep live deployments gated on successful CI and review outstanding browser-test failures before claiming release success.
- Curriculum coverage is judged by mapped learning objectives, not by number of topics or quiz questions.
- Include teaching explanations, practice, applied tasks, feedback and assessments; identify activities that require adult supervision or offline practical work.
- GCSE support must name the chosen awarding bodies, subject specifications, tiers and qualification years; do not claim universal exam-board coverage.
- Run a phase-end gap review; at step 250 independently re-audit and extend beyond 250 if needed.

## Step 1: Complete curriculum inventory and gap analysis

**Deliverable:** a machine-readable coverage inventory of current BrightPath modules, lessons and questions, with explicit unmapped objectives and a review queue.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Inventory existing content without changing live child data.
- Separate evidence of module integration from evidence of statutory objective coverage.
- List missing source references, learning objectives, explanation quality and practical/assessment requirements.
- Establish phase-by-phase traceability and a review process for curriculum accuracy.

## Completion definition

The 250-step roadmap is complete only when its deliverables are reviewed. **Full curriculum coverage** may be claimed only for explicitly listed frameworks and specifications after objective-level mapping, pedagogical review, assessment verification, and accessibility/mobile testing. Any gaps become additional steps.
