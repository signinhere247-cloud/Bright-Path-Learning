# Step 86 of 250 — Upper KS2 geometry: properties of shape

Adds ten supplementary Year 5 and Year 6 lessons covering polygon classification, angle types and angle facts, 3-D shape properties, reflection and translation, accurate 2-D construction, triangle and quadrilateral angles, circle vocabulary, polygon angles and multi-step geometry reasoning.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking, diagrams and protractor/shape-construction activities, and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
