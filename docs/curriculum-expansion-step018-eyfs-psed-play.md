# Step 18 of 250 — EYFS personal, social and emotional development activities

Adds four practical adult-supported activity guides to the existing in-app EYFS selector:
1. **Feelings with puppets** (ACT-EYFS-P2-01): naming feelings, empathy and asking for help.
2. **Try-again challenge** (ACT-EYFS-P2-02): manageable challenges, choice, confidence and flexible problem-solving.
3. **Daily self-care choices** (ACT-EYFS-P2-03): supported handwashing or clothing choices without compulsory independence.
4. **Turn-taking together** (ACT-EYFS-P2-04): cooperative play, consent and kind conflict resolution.

**Eight total guides** now exist across Communication and Language and PSED. Each includes materials, four adult-led steps, prompts, accessible alternatives and safety guidance. Children are not graded for their emotions, behaviour or independence, and no personal disclosures are requested.

These are usable activity guides, not independent proof of statutory EYFS mastery. Child-profile progress integration, educator review, browser accessibility validation and the remaining twenty planned activities are outstanding.
