# Step 62 of 250 — KS1 geography

Adds four adult-supported activities in `src/ks1GeographyActivities.js`. Year 1: drawing a simple fictional map and classifying human/physical features. Year 2: compass directions and map keys, and comparing a UK town with a contrasting non-European locality.

Activities use fictional locations or reviewed source photographs, include four guided steps, reasoning questions and safety/privacy guidance. Place comparisons must avoid stereotypes and use age-appropriate contextualised material.

These are supplementary guides, **not yet connected to live learner navigation**. Real-world fieldwork, countries/continents/oceans, weather patterns and detailed objective-by-objective coverage need further development and educator review.
