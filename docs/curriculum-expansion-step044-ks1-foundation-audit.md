# Step 44 of 250 — KS1 science and foundation source audit

Run `node scripts/ks1-foundation-audit.mjs` to inventory the **actual** subjects, topics and question counts in `src/ks1Foundation.js`. The audit flags missing real-world evidence for science investigations, geography fieldwork, art, computing, design and technology, music and physical education.
