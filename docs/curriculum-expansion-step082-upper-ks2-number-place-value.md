# Step 82 of 250 — Upper KS2 number and place value

Adds ten supplementary Year 5 and Year 6 lessons covering reading and comparing large numbers, powers of ten, rounding, negative numbers, Roman numerals, decimal place-value shifts and multi-step number problems.

Each lesson includes a teaching explanation, two worked examples and five multiple-choice questions with distinct options, answers and explanatory feedback. A year-specific retrieval helper returns independent copies.

Tests validate lesson/question counts, year separation, option uniqueness, answer presence, feedback and retrieval restrictions.

**Review gate:** supplementary content requires educator checking for mathematical accuracy, suitable representations and precise statutory objective mapping. These lessons are not yet connected to learner navigation and completion does not record mastery or assessment evidence.
